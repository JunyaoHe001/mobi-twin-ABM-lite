Privacy and disclosure

Excluded private materials

No original sample personal information is published. The public repository excludes:

  • the original 21,911-row sample table;
  • row-linked exit and destination-probability tables;
  • original individual or donor identifiers;
  • source-row-to-archetype mappings;
  • the private NetLogo model and private inputs;
  • absolute local or institutional file paths;
  • agent-level snapshots or downloadable agent histories;
  • unused source columns or original text fields.

Published materials

The browser receives only aggregate synthetic archetypes, clustered and smoothed profiles, simplified regional geometry, rounded public parameters, and aggregate-output logic.

Disclosure controls

  • Source rows were grouped into 80 synthetic archetypes, with small groups merged before public summaries were produced.
  • Exit curves were reduced to 10 public profiles and destination distributions to 24 public profiles.
  • Weights, category probabilities, age and income summaries, preference summaries, profile mixtures, and public parameters were perturbed and/or coarsely rounded.
  • No source-row mapping is retained in the public package.
  • Only aggregate national trajectories and regional snapshots can be downloaded.
  • Synthetic-agent point locations are not shown.

Limitations

This is an engineering disclosure-reduction workflow, not formal differential privacy and not legal certification of anonymisation. Anything delivered to a browser can be inspected; only materials approved for public visibility are included.
